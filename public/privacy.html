Parallax: privacy & data

Updated November 17, 2026.

Parallax is an Earth-viewing simulation created by Rishit Pradhan. It has no account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

On your device

The iPhone app stores saved views in its own preferences. A saved view contains a name you choose, orbital elements, a recorded time, and camera settings. The app does not upload that library. Deleting the app removes its local data. Rendered images are placed in the app’s cache for sharing when you choose the share action.

Network requests

Current orbital records and imagery use the Parallax service at rp-parallax.vercel.app, CelesTrak, and NASA. Those providers and hosting infrastructure receive ordinary network information, such as IP addresses and request logs. Image requests include dates and the satellite region being viewed. This is not your device’s location: Parallax does not request GPS permission.

Your choices

You can use the bundled historical orbit without a connection, delete saved views, and choose whether to share an image or open an external link. External apps and websites handle shared information under their own policies. The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, health data, or location.

Questions

Use the project’s support page. Avoid posting personal information in public issues.
